Elytra Quick-Swap

One key G equips Elytra and saves your Chestplate with full NBT. Press again to restore. Instant, with inventory-full protection.

⚔

Smart Mace Select

Finds best Mace by hotbar → durability → enchants. Auto-moves to Slot 2 and selects it. Attack instantly — no inventory juggling.

🚀

Rocket Ready

Detects rockets anywhere, optionally centers them to Slot 3. Count shown in HUD. Never rearranges your inventory unnecessarily.

⚡

Combat Mode

Toggle H for ultra-fast flow: Chestplate → Elytra → Fly → Mace. Minimal clicks, maximum impact. Built for 1.21 mace PvP.

🛡

Inventory Safety

Server-validated SWAP/PICKUP clicks, deep copy() of DataComponents, destination checks, and graceful cancel — never lose items.

⚙

Fully Configurable

Every key, slot, HUD position, sound, particle, and message. Edit in-game K → Settings or config/elytra-combat.json.
